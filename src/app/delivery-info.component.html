Delivery Information

Last updated: April 2026

1. Delivery Methods

We offer the following delivery options:

📦 Econt Office

Pick up your order from any Econt office in Bulgaria. When ordering, you can search for the nearest office or use your current location to find the closest one automatically.

🚚 Speedy Office

Pick up your order from any Speedy office in Bulgaria. Similar to Econt, you can search and browse offices on an interactive map during checkout.

🏠 Personal Address

We deliver directly to your door. Just provide a full address during checkout and our courier partner will handle the rest.

2. Delivery Cost

Order Total Delivery Fee

3. Delivery Times

Standard delivery typically takes 1–3 business days within Bulgaria. Delivery times may vary depending on your location and the courier service chosen.

Orders placed before 14:00 on business days are usually dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 14:00 or on weekends/holidays are dispatched on the next business day.

4. Minimum Order

The minimum order value is €50. Orders below this amount cannot proceed to checkout.

5. Payment Options

We accept the following payment methods:

  • Card on Delivery – pay by card when you collect or receive your order
  • Pay Online (myPOS) – secure online card payment via myPOS gateway
  • Pay Online (Revolut) – secure online card payment via Revolut gateway

6. Order Tracking

After your order is dispatched, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information from the selected courier service (Econt or Speedy).

7. Packaging

All wines are carefully packaged to prevent damage during transit. Bottles are individually wrapped and placed in reinforced cardboard boxes. Bag-in-box products (3L) are shipped in their protective outer packaging.

8. Delivery Issues

If your order arrives damaged or if you experience any delivery issues, please contact us within 48 hours at office@ladwine.eu or call +359 12345. Include your order number and photos of any damage.

9. International Delivery

Currently, we only deliver within Bulgaria. We are working on expanding our delivery coverage to other European countries. Subscribe to our newsletter to be notified when international shipping becomes available.
